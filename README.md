**Lantern Duel** — an original 1v1 card duel on the Lantern Isles. You and Mira the Tidecaller each hold 30 life. Spend mana to play creatures and spells from a 24-card deck, strike with your creatures, and take Mira to 0 life before she does the same to you. Mana grows by 1 each turn up to 10. Twelve original cards (creatures with attack/health, spells with targeting), a heuristic AI opponent, WebAudio sound effects generated in code, and win/loss screens with Play Again. No network, no libraries — just static files.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — click **Play**, play a glowing card, click one of your creatures and then an enemy creature or Mira to attack, and press **End Turn** (or Enter) to pass to Mira.
