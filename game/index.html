Lantern Duel

A card duel on the Lantern Isles

You and Mira the Tidecaller each hold 30 life. Spend mana to play creatures and spells, strike with your creatures, and take Mira to 0 life before she does the same to you. Mana grows by 1 each turn, up to 10.

Click a glowing card to play it · click one of your creatures, then an enemy creature or Mira, to attack · End Turn (or Enter) passes to Mira.

An original game · art, sounds and rules made for this duel
